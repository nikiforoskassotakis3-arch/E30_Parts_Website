About E30 Parts Hub

E30 Parts Hub is a web application for BMW E30 owners. Its purpose is to help them organise all the restoration and maintenance jobs on their car in one place.

Features

  • Add, edit, delete and complete jobs with due dates and priorities
  • Filter, sort and live search through your jobs
  • Progress bars and a Kanban board to track your progress
  • Latest activity on the home page
  • A guide to common E30 parts
  • Dark mode
