Maintenance tips

  • Change the timing belt on time. The M20 is an interference engine, so a snapped belt can damage the valves.
  • Replace the water pump together with the timing belt to save labour.
  • Check the oil level every few weeks, especially on older engines.
  • Inspect the brake pads and discs at every service.
  • Look for rust under the battery tray, sills and wheel arches, the common E30 rust spots.
